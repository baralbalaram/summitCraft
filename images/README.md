## Tips for your own photos

- **Landscape**, roughly **1920 × 900 px**. Wider is fine; the banner crops to fit.
- **JPEG under ~400 KB** so pages load quickly on mobile. Free tools like
  squoosh.app can shrink a photo without visible quality loss.
- Keep the subject near the **centre**. The edges may be cropped on phones.
- The banner darkens every photo automatically so the white headline stays
  readable, so bright photos work well.
